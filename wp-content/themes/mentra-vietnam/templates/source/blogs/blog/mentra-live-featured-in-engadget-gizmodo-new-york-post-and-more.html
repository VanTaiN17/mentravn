Batch 1 Sold Out!

Batch 1 is officially sold out! Those lucky enough to be a part of the Founder’s 1000 Club will start to get their Mentra Live beginning the second week of February!

The good news is if you missed out on Batch 1, Batch 2 is on sale now and will ship just a few weeks later in March. However, Batch 2 is also limited to 1,000 Mentra Live, so get yours now!

MentraOS Update

MentraOS is built to make smart glasses feel seamless and powerful from day one. We’re constantly refining features and expanding miniapps so your glasses keep improving the more you use them.

Our co-founder and CTO, Israelov, has been dropping updates on Discord. 👇

Follow our Discord for more updates!

Mentra Live In The News

In our last blog post, we told you about putting out our first press release. We knew we had a good story and a great product, so we’re glad that news outlets and reporters agree! 

We were blown away by the news coverage, which included Engadget, Gizmodo, Digital Trends, International Business Times, 9to5Google, Android Police, New York Post, Complex, and so many more!

 
Please check out our news section in our new website!

We have so many new announcements and updates in the next few weeks. Make sure you're following us!
